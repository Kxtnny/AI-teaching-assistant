LECTURE MEMORY:

**Core Requirements**

* SC1003F-Core, SC1004Core, MH1812Core, CC0003U-Core are core courses for Year 1
* SC3099Core and SC3920F-Core are core courses for Year 3
* Students must complete a Final Year Project (FYP) to be eligible for Highest Distinction

**Major Prescribed Elective (MPE)**

* Students must complete 9 MPE courses, including at least 4 Broadening and Deepening Electives
* MPE courses can be chosen from SC3xxx or SC4xxx
* Students can choose to fulfill the requirement by completing a FYP or taking 3 additional MPE courses

**Pre-requisites**

* SC1003 (co-requisite) with SC1004Core for Year 1
* SC1005Core and SC2000Core for Year 2
* SC2001Core and SC2002Core for Year 2
* SC2007 is a co-requisite for SC2203Core in Year 2

**Career and Innovative Enterprise**

* Students can choose from Broadening and Deepening Electives, such as Introduction to Databases, Sustainability: Society, Economy & Environment, and Year 2 Semester 2 courses

**Common Mistakes**

* Not completing a Final Year Project (FYP) for Highest Distinction
* Failing to complete the required number of Major Prescribed Elective (MPE) courses

**Example Question Types**

* Multiple choice questions on core requirements and MPE courses
* Short-answer questions on pre-requisites and Career and Innovative Enterprise
* Essay questions on the importance of FYP for Highest Distinction

* Not completing a Final Year Project (FYP) for Highest Distinction
