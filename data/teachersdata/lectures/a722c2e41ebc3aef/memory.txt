LECTURE MEMORY:

- The document outlines the curriculum structure for the Bachelor of Computing (Computer Science) program for AY2024-25.
- The curriculum is divided by year and semester, specifying course codes, course types, academic units (AU), and pre-requisites.
- Year 1 focuses on foundational courses such as Calculus, Discrete Mathematics, Linear Algebra, Programming (C and C++), Digital Logic, and introductory computing courses.
- Year 2 includes core courses like Software Engineering, Computer Networks, Operating Systems, Automata, Computability and Complexity, Algorithm Design & Analysis, and electives.
- Year 3 advances to more specialized core courses, major prescribed electives (MPE), and includes a Capstone Project and Professional Internship.
- Year 4 emphasizes Major Prescribed Electives, Broadening and Deepening Electives (BDE), Final Year Project (Part 1 and 2), and continued specialization.
- Total academic units per year range roughly between 35-47 AU.
- Major Prescribed Electives (MPE) must be chosen from SC3xxx/SC4xxx courses, with at least 4 at the 4000-level.
- Students must complete 9 MPE courses in total.
- To be eligible for Highest Distinction, students must complete a Final Year Project.
- Broadening and Deepening Electives (BDE) are included to enhance interdisciplinary knowledge.
- Some courses have co-requisites or pre-requisites, e.g., SC1003 is a pre-requisite for SC1004 and SC1008.
- There are special courses for students who failed QET or have not done Calculus (HW0001, HW0002).
- The curriculum includes courses on ethics, communication, and sustainability to provide a holistic education.
- Internship and capstone projects are integral parts of the later years to provide practical experience.
- The curriculum is structured to progressively build knowledge from foundational to advanced topics in computer science.

Pitfalls:
- Not meeting pre-requisite or co-requisite requirements can delay progression.
- Failure to complete the Final Year Project disqualifies students from Highest Distinction.
- Insufficient selection of MPE courses or failure to meet the 4000-level course requirement may affect graduation eligibility.
