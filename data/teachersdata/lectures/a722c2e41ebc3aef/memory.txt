* It has three semesters in Year 2, two in Year 3, and one in Year 4.

**Important Notes**

* Students must complete a Final Year Project to be eligible for Highest Distinction.
* Students can choose to fulfill the Major Prescribed Elective requirement by completing a Final Year Project or taking 3 additional MPE courses.

Common Mistakes/Confusing Points:

* Ensure that all Core, Co-requisite, and MPE courses are completed according to the course structure.
* Make sure to complete the required number of BDE courses and MPE courses for each year.
* Pay attention to the requirements for completing a Final Year Project.
