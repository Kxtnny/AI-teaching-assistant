Document Summary:

* The Bachelor of Computing (Computer Science) program has two main components: Core courses and Major Prescribed Electives (MPE).
* Core courses include SC1003F-Core, SC1004Core, SC1005Core, MH1812Core, CC0003U-Core, CC0001U-Core, CC0002U CORE, and others.
* MPE courses are categorized into two groups: SC3xxx/SC4xxx (Major Prescribe Elective) and MPE (Choose within SC4xxx).
* Students must complete 9 MPE courses, including at least 4 Broadening and Deepening Electives.
* The program also includes a Final Year Project (FYP), which is required for Highest Distinction.
