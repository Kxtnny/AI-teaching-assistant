**Summary of the Academic Unit Requirement for Bachelor of Computing (Computer Science)**

The Bachelor of Computing (Computer Science) curriculum requires students to complete a total of 135 academic units (AU) over four years. The curriculum is structured into four main components: Core, Major Prescribed Elective (MPE), Broadening and Deepening Elective (BDE), and Career & Innovative Enterprise for the Future World.
