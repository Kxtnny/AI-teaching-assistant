**Summary:**
- The curriculum outlines courses and academic unit requirements for Bachelor of Computing (Computer Science).  
- Year 1 includes foundational courses like Calculus, Programming, and Discrete Mathematics.  
- Year 2 covers core subjects such as Software Engineering, Computer Networks, and Algorithm Design.  
- Year 3 and 4 focus on Major Prescribed Electives (MPE), Broadening and Deepening Electives (BDE), and Final Year Projects.  
- Students must complete 9 MPE courses and either a Final Year Project or 3 additional MPEs for graduation.

**Key Terms:**
Academic Unit, Core Course, Major Prescribed Elective, Broadening and Deepening Elective, Final Year Project, Pre-requisite, Co-requisite, Year Standing, Capstone Project, Professional Internship, Calculus, Software Engineering

**Action Items:**
- Review the course prerequisites and plan your semester schedule accordingly.  
- Explore the CSC MPE courses and specializations on the CCDS website for elective choices.  
- Prepare for the Final Year Project to qualify for the Highest Distinction award.
