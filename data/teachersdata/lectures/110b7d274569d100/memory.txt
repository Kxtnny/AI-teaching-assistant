LECTURE MEMORY: Transforming Digital Experiences

- Past systems were fragmented with multiple logins and non-standard interfaces for customers.
- Customers had to manually book broadband appointments; enterprises relied on field engineers for site surveys.
- Channel partners manually booked visits; applications were siloed across portals.
- Assistance was primarily via phone calls.
- Present systems feature unified single sign-on with multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Customers use self-service portals with service discovery; enterprises have centralized incident tracking.
- Interfaces for enterprise and customers are aligned in design.
- Root Cause Analysis (RCA) is performed post-incident.
- Suggested improvements include AI-driven network log analysis for proactive issue detection.
- Real-time billing validation is recommended to prevent overcharges.
- Emphasis on accurate and auditable revenue reporting.
- Future vision includes AI-based recommender systems tailored to business needs.
- Integration of AI into automated marketing workflows.
- Agentic AI to handle large query volumes with intelligent, context-aware responses.
- Enhanced self-service capabilities powered by AI.
- Consumer-facing app: MySingtel; Enterprise portal: Empower Portal with 300+ products.
- Internal teams provide support when self-service is insufficient.
- Project context involves preparing SWORD analysis slides and conducting market analysis focusing on competitive metrics, customer personas, and innovation.

Pitfalls:
- Fragmented systems cause poor customer experience and inefficiency.
- Manual processes increase operational costs and delays.
- Lack of proactive issue detection leads to reactive customer support.
- Inaccurate billing and revenue reporting can damage trust and compliance.
- Over-reliance on human agents limits scalability.

Example Problem Types:
- Designing unified digital platforms to replace fragmented legacy systems.
- Implementing AI for proactive network issue detection.
- Developing self-service portals with integrated AI assistance.
- Creating AI recommender systems for personalized business suggestions.
- Conducting market and competitive analysis using frameworks like SWORD.
