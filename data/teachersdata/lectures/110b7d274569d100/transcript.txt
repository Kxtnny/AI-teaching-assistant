Transforming Digital Experiences - Meeting Summary
1. The Past: Fragmented Systems
Customers:
- Multiple logins for different services.
- Non-standard, non-design-centric interfaces.
- Manual broadband appointment booking.
Enterprise:
- Field engineers required for physical site surveys.
- Channel partners manually booked customer visits.
- Siloed applications across different portals.
- Assistance required via phone calls.
2. The Present: Unified, Automated, Assisted
Customers:
- Single sign-on with MFA.
- Self-service portals with service discovery features.
- Automated processes; live agent can assist by phone.
Enterprise:
- Central incident tracking system (specific tool not mentioned).
- Similar interface design to customer portals.
- Root Cause Analysis performed after incidents.
3. Suggestions for Improvement
- Use network logs with AI to detect issues before customer reports.
- Real-time billing validation to prevent overcharges.
- Ensure revenue reporting is accurate and auditable.
4. Future: AI and Agentic Capabilities
- AI-based recommender system for business-specific suggestions.
- Integration into automated marketing workflows.
- Agentic AI for handling large query volumes with intelligent responses.
- Self-service capabilities enhanced by AI.
Consumer: MySingtel App for mobile plans.
Enterprise: Empower Portal for over 300 products.
Internal teams assist where self-service is insufficient.
