**Summary:**
- Large Language Models (LLMs) generate natural language responses using transformer architectures.  
- Tokens are embedded into vectors to capture semantic relationships in text.  
- Prompt engineering customizes LLM outputs by providing specific contextual instructions.  
- Techniques like Automatic Prompt Engineer (APE) and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) improve reasoning accuracy.  
- The proposed tool refines user prompts to reduce hallucinations and enhance educational chatbot reliability.
