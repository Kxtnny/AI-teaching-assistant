**Summary:**
- Large Language Models (LLMs) use transformer architectures to generate natural language responses.  
- Prompts critically influence LLM output accuracy and behavior, requiring careful engineering.  
- Automatic Prompt Engineer (APE) and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting improve reasoning accuracy.  
- Existing prompting methods often need manual design, challenging non-expert users.  
- The proposed tool refines user prompts to enhance reliability and reduce hallucinations in educational chatbots.
