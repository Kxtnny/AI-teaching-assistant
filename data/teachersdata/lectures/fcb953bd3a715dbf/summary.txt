**Summary:**
- Large Language Models (LLMs) generate natural language responses using transformer-based architectures.  
- Tokens are converted into embeddings to capture semantic relationships in text.  
- Prompt engineering customizes LLM outputs by providing contextual instructions and constraints.  
- Automatic Prompt Engineer (APE) and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting improve reasoning accuracy.  
- The proposed tool refines user prompts to enhance reliability and reduce hallucinations in educational chatbots.  

**Key Terms:**
Large Language Models, transformer architecture, tokens, embeddings, prompt engineering, Automatic Prompt Engineer, Chain-of-Thought, Active Prompting, hallucinations, zero-shot learning, reasoning benchmarks, educational chatbots
