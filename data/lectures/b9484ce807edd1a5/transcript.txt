Kendall-Lee Notation for Queuing Systems
A/B/k  is used to describe various queuing systems where   
A identifies the arrival distribution, 
B  the service (departure) distribution and 
k  the number of channels for the system.
  
Symbols used for the arrival and service processes are: 
 M - Markov distributions (Poisson/exponential),
 D - Deterministic (constant) and 
 G - General distribution (with a known mean and 
variance).
