**Summary:**
- Decision analysis includes criteria like maximax, maximin, minimax regret, and expected value.
- Decision trees graphically represent decisions, chance events, and payoffs over time.
- Bayes Theorem updates prior probabilities based on new information like market research.
- Expected Value of Perfect Information (EVPI) and Sampled Information (EVSI) quantify value of information.
- Optimal strategies depend on comparing survey costs to EVSI and revising decisions accordingly.

**Key Terms:**
Decision analysis, decision tree, payoff table, maximax, maximin, minimax regret, expected value, Bayes Theorem, EVPI, EVSI, prior probability, market research

**Action Items:**
- Practice constructing and evaluating decision trees with different payoff and probability scenarios.
- Apply Bayes Theorem to update probabilities using new data or research findings.
- Calculate EVPI and EVSI to determine when acquiring additional information is cost-effective.
