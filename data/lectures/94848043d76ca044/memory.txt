LECTURE MEMORY:

- Shift towards Education 4.0 emphasizes active learning over passive learning.
- Active learning engages students through participation (e.g., project-based learning).
- Passive learning relies on listening and observation without deep engagement.
- Core benefits of active learning:
  1. Improves communication skills
  2. Enhances understanding of material
  3. Encourages critical thinking
  4. Boosts academic performance, especially in STEM
  5. Promotes collaboration among students
- Challenges in implementing active learning:
  - Knowledge gaps hinder meaningful explanations
  - Peer evaluations can be ineffective or inaccurate
  - Resource-intensive, requiring significant instructor time and supervision
- AI in education offers key benefits:
  - Instant support with explanations and content summaries
  - Enables self-paced, customized learning progression
  - Improves accessibility and learning efficiency
  - Supports academic tasks like drafting, research, and structuring
- Critical limitations of AI in education:
  - Encourages passive learning by providing ready answers
  - Risk of hallucinations: AI may generate inaccurate or biased info
  - Oversimplification of complex topics, losing depth
  - Lack of validation or challenge to student misconceptions
- Core problem:
  - Active learning is effective but hard to scale due to resource demands
  - AI assistants are scalable but promote passive learning habits
- Proposed solution: Feynman Technique to combine benefits
  - Pick a concept
  - Explain it simply
  - Identify knowledge gaps
  - Simplify and refine understanding
- Feynman Technique fosters active learning with AI support, encouraging reasoning and conceptual mastery.

Pitfalls:
- Over-reliance on AI can reduce critical thinking.
- AI hallucinations can mislead learners.
- Peer evaluation without proper guidance may propagate errors.
- Active learning requires resources that may not be scalable.

Example Problem Types:
- Explaining complex STEM concepts in simple terms.
- Identifying gaps in understanding through self-explanation.
- Collaborative projects requiring peer feedback.
- Using AI tools to draft and research while maintaining active engagement.
