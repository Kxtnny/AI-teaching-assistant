**Summary:**
- Active learning engages students actively, improving communication and critical thinking skills.  
- AI in education offers instant support and personalized learning but risks promoting passive learning.  
- Active learning is effective but hard to scale due to high instructor involvement.  
- AI assistants are scalable but may encourage over-reliance on generated answers.  
- The Feynman Technique helps deepen understanding by simplifying concepts and identifying knowledge gaps.  

**Key Terms:**
Active Learning, Passive Learning, Feynman Technique, AI in Education, Critical Thinking, Peer Evaluation, Academic Performance, Instant Support, Self-Paced Learning, Hallucinations, Oversimplification, Knowledge Gap  

**Action Items:**
- Practice the Feynman Technique by explaining concepts simply and identifying gaps in understanding.  
- Use AI tools to supplement learning but critically evaluate AI-generated information.  
- Engage in active learning methods like project-based learning to enhance collaboration and comprehension.
