**Summary:**
- Active learning engages students through participation and improves communication and critical thinking.  
- Challenges of active learning include knowledge gaps, peer evaluation issues, and high resource demands.  
- AI in education offers instant support, personalized pacing, and improved accessibility for learners.  
- AI limitations include promoting passive learning, hallucinations, oversimplification, and lack of validation.  
- The Feynman Technique combines active learning with AI scalability by simplifying concepts and identifying knowledge gaps.  

**Key Terms:**
Active Learning, Passive Learning, Feynman Technique, AI in Education, Critical Thinking, Peer Evaluation, Knowledge Gap, Instant Support, Academic Performance, Oversimplification, Hallucinations, Self-Paced Learning
