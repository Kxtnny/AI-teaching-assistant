AI in Education: Critical Limitations
Passive Learning
Over-reliance encourages students to 
passively absorb AI-generated 
answers rather than thinking 
critically.
Hallucinations
Generative models can confidently 
produce inaccurate, biased, or 
entirely fabricated information.
Oversimplification
Complex, nuanced topics are often 
reduced to generic summaries, 
stripping away necessary depth.
Lack of Validation
AI often fails to challenge the user's 
misconceptions or validate the depth 
of their actual understanding.
