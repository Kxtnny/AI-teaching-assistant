**Summary:**
- Fuzzy logic uses membership functions to represent subjective degrees of truth between 0 and 1.  
- Responsibility Sensitive Safety (RSS) defines formal safety rules for autonomous vehicles to avoid accidents.  
- Exposure Time marks when an object becomes visible to a vehicle, critical for safe driving decisions.  
- Unreasonable situations arise from excessive speed or improper behavior of other agents in traffic.  
- Fuzzy sets can be discrete or continuous and are manipulated using set-theoretic operations like union and intersection.  

**Key Terms:**
Fuzzy logic, Membership function, Responsibility Sensitive Safety, Exposure Time, Unreasonable Situation, Danger Threshold, Fuzzy set, Trapezoidal MF, Fuzzy partition, Convexity, Set-theoretic operations, Semantic Action Space
