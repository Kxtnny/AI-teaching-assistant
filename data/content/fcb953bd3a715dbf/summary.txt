**Summary:**
- Large Language Models (LLMs) generate natural language responses using transformer architectures.  
- Prompts critically influence LLM output quality and model behavior.  
- Prompt engineering customizes LLM responses by providing contextual instructions.  
- Techniques like Automatic Prompt Engineer (APE) and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) improve reasoning accuracy.  
- The paper proposes a tool combining APE and Active CoT to enhance educational chatbot reliability.  

**Key Terms:**
Large Language Models, transformer architecture, tokens, embeddings, prompt engineering, Automatic Prompt Engineer, Chain-of-Thought, Active Prompting, zero-shot learning, hallucinations, educational chatbots, reasoning benchmarks
